## 1. Why this project exists

**Working objective.** Predict continuous *in vitro* CFTR functional rescue for untested variants and treatments, test whether knowledge transfers across modulators, and identify when predictions become unreliable—especially across selectively sampled research cohorts.

The target is **functional rescue in percentage points (pp) of wild-type CFTR function**, not a binary FDA eligibility label, clinical treatment efficacy, or the response of an individual patient. This distinction must remain explicit throughout the project.

### Evolving scientific question

The starting question was whether sequence-based models or frozen protein language models can predict **ETI** response at unseen CFTR mutation positions. Experiments then expanded to **VTI** response and ETI→VTI transfer. The strongest current observation is **large, reproducible cohort-dependent prediction bias**, while the tested transfer and model-disagreement methods do **not** robustly solve it.

The working research hypothesis is therefore:

> Response prediction with heterogeneous CFTR functional datasets is limited by selection, study effects, and treatment-associated shift—not merely by the expressiveness of mutation embeddings.

This is a **hypothesis**, not yet an established causal mechanism or a demonstrated novel method.

### Bihler 2024 ETI

- 661 DMSO and 659 ELX+TEZ records, paired at variant-cDNA level.
- Five unresolved identifier groups were excluded from the initial unambiguous pairing. These include duplicate/alternate control results (F508del, G551D, R1066H, H1085R) and wild-type control records. They were not blindly averaged.
- 652 complete paired entries comprise 586 validated missense variants and 66 other variant types. These totals are **not** equivalent to 652 independent eligible missense observations.
- Bihler's experimental WT background carries **M470**, while canonical UniProt P13569 carries **V470**. For the 2024 missense features and validation, the project reconstructs the correct assay background. M470V must not be removed as a reference mismatch.

### Bihler 2026 VTI

- 419 DMSO and 418 `VX121+VX661` condition records; **415** usable paired responses.
- 271 pairs are annotated in the VTI-only study stratum and 144 as `ETI, VTI`.
- Exact cross-study cDNA overlap is **145**, i.e. 144 annotated `ETI, VTI` plus one annotated `VTI`. There are 270 2026-only cDNA identifiers.
- Parsed variant types: 357 missense with parseable positions; 53 non-missense; 5 unparseable missense annotations. In the transfer setup, 295 eligible missense variants are development candidates and 120 further records are excluded for protection/eligibility reasons.
- The previously ETI-characterised subset was deliberately enriched for variants with poor/borderline ETI responsiveness. It is **not** a population-representative external test.
- The in-vitro **VTI** assay used vanzacaftor/tezacaftor/**ivacaftor**; it should not be confused with the clinical combination using **deutivacaftor**.

## 3. Outcomes and experimental definitions

All response values below are in **pp of WT CFTR activity**.

| Derived outcome | Definition |
|---|---|
| `eti_rescue_pp` | `(ELX/TEZ condition, FSK+IVA %WT)` minus `(DMSO condition, FSK %WT)` |
| VTI total rescue | `(VNZ/TEZ condition, FSK+IVA %WT)` minus `(DMSO condition, FSK %WT)` |
| ETI corrector-associated increment | `(ELX/TEZ condition, FSK+IVA %WT)` minus `(DMSO condition, FSK+IVA %WT)` |
| VTI corrector-associated increment | `(VNZ/TEZ condition, FSK+IVA %WT)` minus `(DMSO condition, FSK+IVA %WT)` |

The corrector-associated increment is **not** a mechanistically isolated effect of one compound; pharmacological interactions are possible. Cross-study differences also include study/batch effects and should **not** be presented as randomized comparative drug efficacy.

## 5. Experiments completed and results

### 5.1 ETI response from mutation sequence features

Training-only CV: **3 repeats × 5 position-disjoint folds**, 422 missense training examples.

| Model | Position-averaged MAE (pp) |
|---|---:|
| Sequence Random Forest | **22.983** |
| Sequence Forest, no numerical position | 23.451 |
| Sequence Ridge | 23.826 |
| Position-only Random Forest | 24.035 |
| Median | 24.128 |
| Position Ridge | 24.320 |

Forest improvement over median: **+1.145 pp** (95% position-bootstrap CI **+0.091 to +2.209**). Forest improvement over sequence Ridge: +0.843 pp (CI −0.035 to +1.729), inconclusive.

Initial 56-variant exploratory validation: Forest **23.246 pp position MAE**, variant MAE 22.825 pp, RMSE 26.899 pp, Spearman **0.447**. This is **modest** predictive signal, not precision theratyping.

### 5.2 Frozen ESM-2 benchmark

- Frozen [`facebook/esm2_t12_35M_UR50D`](https://huggingface.co/facebook/esm2_t12_35M_UR50D), resolved model revision `6fbf070e65b0b7291e7bbcd451118c216cff79d8`.
- Mutation-centered **801-aa** WT and mutant windows on the M470 background; **480 features**, **478** development variants embedded. Original 108 ETI test variants were excluded.
- Representations: WT, mutant, mutant−WT, WT+delta, delta+handcrafted sequence.

| Representation | Training CV position MAE | Exploratory validation position MAE |
|---|---:|---:|
| **ESM WT (CV-selected)** | **22.587** | 24.269 |
| ESM mutant | 22.838 | 23.780 |
| ESM delta | 23.714 | 25.170 |
| ESM WT + delta | 22.881 | **23.130** |
| ESM delta + sequence | 23.263 | 23.670 |

CV-selected ESM WT was **1.023 pp worse** than the Forest on validation (paired improvement −1.023 pp, 95% CI **−3.953 to +2.146**). The best-looking validation-only ESM variant (`WT + delta`) was **not CV-selected** and should not be retroactively promoted as the winner.

**Conclusion:** Frozen ESM-2 has no demonstrated reliable advantage here. WT context helps encode position-level information but does not alone encode which alternative residue is introduced.

### 5.3 ETI→VTI total-rescue transfer

Training-only CV: **2 repeats × 5 position-disjoint folds** on 210 VTI training variants; ETI source training positions are restricted within each fold.

| Model | CV position MAE (pp) |
|---|---:|
| VTI-only Forest | **17.871** |
| Pooled ETI+VTI Forest | 18.214 |
| Median | 18.371 |
| VTI Ridge | 19.411 |
| ETI zero-shot Forest | 22.150 |

The pooled model did not outperform VTI-only learning. On the original 52-variant exploratory VTI validation set, Forest position MAE was **15.238 pp**, worse than the median (**14.890 pp**). Thus no robust transfer improvement has been demonstrated.

### 5.4 Training-only matched assays

**66 exact cDNA-matched variants at 56 positions.** Cross-study comparison:

| Measurement | Spearman ρ | 95% position-bootstrap CI | Median absolute shift (pp) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| DMSO FSK baseline | **0.793** | [0.644, 0.889] | **1.414** |
| DMSO FSK+IVA | **0.822** | [0.685, 0.903] | **1.897** |
| Total modulator rescue | 0.658 | [0.482, 0.780] | 6.668 |
| Corrector increment | 0.624 | [0.453, 0.745] | 5.394 |

**Interpretation:** Baseline phenotype rankings are reasonably consistent, while treatment-associated response agreement is weaker. This cannot isolate treatment-specific effects from assay/study effects.

### 5.5 Corrector-associated ETI→VTI transfer

| Model | CV position MAE (pp) |
|---|---:|
| VTI-only Forest | **15.889** |
| Median | 15.922 |
| Pooled Forest | 16.286 |
| VTI Ridge | 17.445 |
| ETI zero-shot Forest | 17.956 |

Forest gain over median: **+0.033 pp** (CI **−1.037 to +1.116**). Pooling instead of VTI-only Forest: **−0.397 pp** (CI −1.358 to +0.655). Mechanistic decomposition alone did **not** solve transfer.

### 5.6 Cohort selection and directional prediction bias

Training VTI cohorts:

| Cohort | n | Median VTI total rescue |
|---|---:|---:|
| Previously ETI-characterised (`ETI, VTI`) | 66 | **13.476 pp** |
| VTI-only | 144 | **39.555 pp** |

The **26.079 pp** gap is descriptive and heavily influenced by differing recruitment/selection rules. In training-only out-of-fold predictions:

| Target | VTI Forest bias, ETI-precharacterised | VTI Forest bias, VTI-only |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total rescue | **+14.446 pp** | **−5.161 pp** |
| Corrector increment | **+12.623 pp** | **−4.651 pp** |

Bias is predicted minus observed. For total rescue, position-clustered CIs are [+9.98, +18.90] and [−9.29, −1.52] respectively. The pooled model tends to improve bias for the VTI-only cohort but can worsen it in the ETI-precharacterised cohort. **Aggregate error hides strong cohort heterogeneity.** The data do not yet establish the cause.

### 5.7 Reliability: disagreement is not enough

Tested whether disagreement between pooled and VTI-only Forests ranked difficult variants. Results from train-only OOF predictions:

| Target | Spearman(disagreement, absolute error) | Position MAE at 25% lowest disagreement | Full-coverage position MAE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total rescue | **−0.070** | 19.943 pp | **17.871 pp** |
| Corrector increment | **+0.011** | 18.135 pp | **15.889 pp** |

Risk–coverage was nonmonotonic. **Do not claim calibrated confidence, useful abstention, or conformal coverage from this score.** These are exploratory diagnostics, not prospective guarantees.

## 6. Conclusions we can defend now

1. Sequence-only models recover **modest** position-held-out signal for ETI, but errors remain large.
2. Frozen ESM-2 was **not reliably superior** to a small sequence-feature Random Forest.
3. Zero-shot ETI transfer and naïve pooled ETI/VTI training did **not** reliably outperform VTI-only baselines.
4. Baseline FSK phenotypes are **more consistent across studies** than modulator-related rescue signals.
5. **Cohort-dependent error and opposing prediction bias** are substantial and replicated across total-rescue and corrector-increment targets.
6. Forest-model disagreement did **not** provide useful error ranking on the explored training data.

**Not established:** causal mechanism for bias, demonstrated method to remove shift, clinical therapeutic benefit, independent patient-derived validation, model superiority on frozen test sets, or a confirmed literature-novel contribution.

No bulk cleanup of `data/`, `results/`, or `logs/`; no `git clean -fdx`, recursive removal of entire scientific directories, or renaming modules while imports work.

Before pushing, verify `git status --short`, inspect `.gitignore`, and commit only `src/`, `tests/`, `scripts/`, `docs/`, `pyproject.toml`, and intentional `data/manifests/` changes. Never rely on GitHub as a backup for ignored raw or processed data.

## 10. Next scientific phase (proposed, not yet conducted)

**A. Cohort-shift characterisation (train-only).** Compare mutation types, protein domains, baseline functional phenotypes, and structural novelty across VTI study cohorts. Quantify overlap and selection effects; baseline activity may be used as an explanatory covariate, **not** as a feature for a strict sequence-only deployable model unless the use case provides it at inference.

**B. Reliability under grouped sampling.** Establish honest position-level out-of-fold calibration and compare residual-scale, training-neighbourhood, and structural-distance uncertainty scores. Predeclare evaluation metrics, including position-weighted MAE, subgroup bias, risk–coverage, interval coverage and subgroup coverage. Conformal guarantees cannot be assumed under arbitrary cohort selection shift.

**C. Pre-register final evaluation.** Avoid further validation cherry-picking. Use frozen ETI/VTI tests once with a selected, prespecified model and confidence-interval protocol; keep explicit controls for retrospective study selection.

**D. Literature and external validation.** Check closest CFTR functional/theratyping predictors and uncertainty methods for genuine novelty. Establish whether any truly open, independent patient-derived assay measurements are accessible without restricted credentials or overlapping variants.
